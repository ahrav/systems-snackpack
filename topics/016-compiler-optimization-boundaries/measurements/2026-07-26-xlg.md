The imported/local timing is a failed performance negative control:

the helper implementation. The imported/local geometric mean was 0.8144 in

invalidates a causal reading of the pooled inner ratio. The whole-process
imported/local ratio was 1.0115 [`0.9987`, `1.0242`]. Preserve this discrepancy;
do not normalize it away or claim a speedup.
