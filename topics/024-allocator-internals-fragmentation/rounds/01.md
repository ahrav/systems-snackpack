# Round 1: Equal live bytes, different reclaimability

Date: 2026-08-03

## Question

How much post-trim memory remains when equal live allocations occupy a compact
prefix or remain scattered through one glibc arena?

## Design

- Treatment application: one fresh process.
- Assignment: 12 predeclared four-period `ABBA` or `BAAB` blocks.
- Analysis unit: one complete four-process block contrast.
- Subsamples: allocation-loop operations and phase snapshots inside a process.
- A/B stopping: 12 complete blocks, or 48 processes.
- A/A diagnostic: four complete blocks with both labels mapped to compact.
- Estimand: scattered/compact post-trim RSS geometric ratio.
- Interval: two-sided 95% Student-t interval over paired log contrasts.
- Timing: allocation, free sweep, and `malloc_trim(0)` have separate clocks.

Both treatments retain 16,384 of 262,144 allocations and 4,194,304 requested
bytes. The probe aborts when allocator controls, survivor counts, payload
checks, or checksums fail.

## Interpretation

Equal `uordblks` with different arena and anonymous RSS supports an
address-layout fragmentation mechanism. It does not prove a general allocator
retention rate. `malloc_trim(0)` returning `1` reports that some memory was
released; it does not report the released byte count.

The checked-in candidate requires final runs on the required AArch64 host and
the runtime-resolved `xxl` x86-64 host. The measurement notes remain pending
until those exact-source receipts pass validation.
