bytes, and 6,557,696 `uordblks` bytes. Median post-trim RSS was 8,076 KiB for
compact and 73,348 KiB for scattered. The paired scattered/compact estimate
was 9.0826 with a 95% t interval of [9.0809, 9.0842] across 12 block
contrasts. The paired difference was 65,272.2 KiB [65,270.8, 65,273.5].

was 6,561,792 versus 73,400,320 bytes. Before explicit trimming, median RSS was
73,352 KiB in both treatments. `malloc_trim(0)` returned one in every row.

The allocation-loop ratio was 0.9967 [0.9868, 1.0068]. Startup and procfs
snapshots were outside that clock. The compact/compact A/A RSS ratio was
0.99994 [0.99927, 1.00061].

The final ELF was a dynamically linked ELF64 executable that needed only
`libc.so.6`. The final x86-64 image contained PLT calls to `calloc`, `malloc`,
`memset`, `free`, `mallinfo2`, `malloc_trim`, and `malloc_usable_size`.
