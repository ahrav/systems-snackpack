## What the timing tests

Each process creates and pins two workers before timing. The timer includes the
start barrier, 10,000,000 relaxed atomic increments per worker, the final CPU
checks, and the done barrier. It excludes process startup, worker creation,
joins, and teardown. The primary schedule contains eight four-process
ABBA/BAAB blocks. Four additional blocks compare padded A with padded B.

All 48 fresh processes passed final-count, layout, affinity, source, runner, and
binary checks. The 16 packed processes had a descriptive median timed interval
of 361,560,148 nanoseconds, with a range of 312,236,274 to 465,887,673. The 16
primary padded processes had a median of 66,133,716 nanoseconds, with a range of
65,088,972 to 87,738,287.

## Generated code and mechanism boundary

The measured binary's stable loop contains eight-way-unrolled x86-64 `lock
incq` instructions plus a scalar tail. This proves the emitted locked atomic
read-modify-write lowering. It does not reveal snoop counts, directory activity,
or cache-to-cache routing.

`perf c2c record -e list` exited 255 with `memory events not supported`. No
sampled cache-to-cache or snoop-hit-modified evidence is available. The measured
claim is limited to a layout-sensitive elapsed-time difference for atomic
increments on this host.
