## Steady-loop measurements

CPU 3 ran one fresh pinned process at a time. Each ordering comparison used 12
blocks and four processes per block, with alternating ABBA and BAAB order. The
A/A control used two consecutive processes in each of 12 blocks. The estimate
is the median within-block geometric-mean ratio; the IQR and range cover
process blocks. Setup and a separate warmup were recorded but excluded from
elapsed time. The raw median elapsed costs were 0.156795 ns for Relaxed store,
0.156804 ns for Release store, 5.327692 ns for SeqCst store, 5.680918 ns for
Relaxed `fetch_add`, and 5.687874 ns for SeqCst `fetch_add`.
