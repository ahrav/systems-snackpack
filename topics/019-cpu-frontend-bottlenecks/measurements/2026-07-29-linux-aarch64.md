## Observed final image and counters

Both images contain 512 eight-byte leaves with identical
`add x0, x0, #1; ret` instructions. Leaf starts are 16 bytes apart in the dense
image and 4096 bytes apart in the sparse image. The `.text` section grew from
9,204 to 2,097,428 bytes.

The linked `run_rounds` code is not byte-identical: the dense image contains
three loop-entry `nop` instructions and is 116 bytes; the sparse image contains
two and is 112 bytes. This is observed downstream code-generation movement from
the alignment treatment. It is another reason to describe the comparison as a
final-layout treatment rather than a pure cache-capacity experiment.

Each PMU pass used four balanced blocks and eight fresh processes per arm. All
48 attempts completed and all counts ran at 100%.

## Interpretation

Measured facts establish a 3.37-fold workload slowdown, a 228-fold `.text`
increase, equal leaf instructions, nearly equal retired instructions, and
large increases in the recorded L1I refill and instruction-TLB walk events on
this host.

The treatment changes instruction-cache footprint, translation footprint,
branch-target spacing, prefetch conditions, and one caller padding instruction
together. The event movement supports a frontend-footprint explanation. It
does not assign a causal share to any one structure or establish a property of
other Arm or AArch64 processors.
