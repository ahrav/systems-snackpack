## Observed final image and counters

Both images contain 512 five-byte leaves. Leaf starts are 16 bytes apart in the
dense image and 4096 bytes apart in the sparse image. The `.text` section grew
from 9,053 to 2,097,405 bytes. Focused disassembly shows the same
`lea 1(%rdi), %rax; ret` leaf and the same `run_rounds` instruction sequence,
apart from addresses and displacements.

Each PMU pass used four balanced blocks and eight fresh processes per arm. All
reported counts below ran at 100%.

The anchor and Intel delivery groups completed. The generic L1I aliases,
per-thread generic instruction-TLB group, and
`frontend_retired.{l1i,itlb}_miss` group failed on every attempted process.
Their exact errors and all 80 PMU attempt records remain in the raw archive.

## Interpretation

Measured facts establish a 7.38-fold workload slowdown, a 232-fold `.text`
increase, equal leaf instructions, nearly equal retired instructions, and a
large shift from DSB-delivered to MITE-delivered operations on this host.

The experiment does not isolate decoded-operation-cache capacity. Alignment
also changes instruction-cache footprint, instruction translation,
branch-target spacing, and prefetch conditions. Naming the DSB-to-MITE delivery
shift is an observed event relationship; assigning the full elapsed-time change
to that shift is an inference.
