# Round 1: scalar filtering and deletion history

The independent ordered-map oracle checks map semantics. Plain and tagged
variants use identical placement and retain identical probe paths. Instrumented
untimed queries distinguish control reads from full key equality work.

The declared experiment measures warm lookup throughput after setup. Same-size
rebuilding changes the retained table state; its cost is excluded and therefore
cannot establish whether paying for a rebuild is profitable.

See [commands and controls](../README.md) and [measurements](../measurements/results.md).
