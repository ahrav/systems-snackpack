# Round 1: preservation and retention

A collector scans A before an application moves B's child C into A and clears B.
Tracing without barriers misses C. Shading the inserted or overwritten reference
preserves C. SATB also retains objects deleted during the cycle.

The independent closure oracle checks preservation across small graphs and schedules.
Repeated simulator workloads quantify scans, barrier calls, retained objects and
wall time under declared controls. Results and exact source identity are retained
in the topic directory. Production latency and real concurrent execution require
runtime-specific experiments.
