# Round 1: the experimental unit

## Decision rule

Name the estimand before choosing the harness. Cold start, hot execution, and
latency under offered load are different quantities.

Choose the outer replicate from the claim:

- Exact linked images with runtime variation: fresh processes.
- Compiler or layout variation: independent builds.
- Host-population claims: hosts selected through a population-valid process.

Inner iterations reduce timer noise. They do not create process-level degrees
of freedom.

## A/B protocol

Place one `A` and one `B` process in each short temporal block. Randomize their
order and enforce equal `AB` and `BA` counts. Analyze one paired difference or
log ratio per block.

Keep these boundaries separate:

- correctness versus speed;
- process startup versus timed work;
- fixed hot-state protocol versus demonstrated stationarity;
- elapsed time versus explanatory counters;
- observed code generation versus causal mechanism;
- exact-host evidence versus architecture-wide inference.

## Negative control

The experiment assigns different labels to identical code. Fixed-order
estimates expose position bias. A reciprocal multiplicative position effect
cancels to `1`.

If the negative control reports a material label effect, reject the harness.
Do not use it to judge a smaller optimization.
