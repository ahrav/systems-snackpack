## Measured result

The table reports arithmetic mean mebibytes per second and sample standard
deviation (SD) across 12 fresh processes. One mebibyte is 1,048,576 bytes.
Inner codec repetitions only lengthen each process interval.

| Structured zstd level 1 | Stored bytes | Encode MiB/s, mean ± SD | Decode MiB/s, mean ± SD |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1,024 independent 256-byte units | 101,652 | 71.154 ± 0.094 | 179.878 ± 0.716 |
| One 262,144-byte batch | 1,642 | 4,763.990 ± 39.966 | 11,001.446 ± 58.167 |

Within each complete block, the batched-to-independent time-per-byte ratio was
`0.014936` for encode with log-ratio SD `0.008571`, and `0.016350` for decode
with log-ratio SD `0.006958` (`n=12`). The working-model intervals were
`[0.014855, 0.015018]` and `[0.016278, 0.016423]`. These descriptive intervals
assume independent, approximately normal block log contrasts; sequential work
on one shared host does not establish those assumptions.

LZ4 stored 102,127 independent-unit bytes and 5,284 batch bytes. For the
deterministic pseudorandom control, both codecs selected raw payloads for every
unit: 275,456 stored bytes in independent form and 262,157 in batch form after
the common 13-byte-per-unit header.

The separate no-work process control averaged 2.265 ms in 12 processes before
the timed schedule and 2.373 ms in 12 after it. Startup was not subtracted from
the in-process codec intervals.

## Interpretation limit

This is a hot, single-thread, pinned-processor, in-memory microbenchmark on this
host. It excludes input/output (I/O), cold allocation, dictionaries, contention, tail
latency, energy, and hostile-input exhaustion. The unit-shape treatment changes
call count, framing, history reset, and fallback together. It does not identify
one of those mechanisms in isolation or establish an Arm-family result.
