Within each complete block, the batched-to-independent time-per-byte ratio was
`0.014304` for encode with log-ratio SD `0.007510`, and `0.014560` for decode
with log-ratio SD `0.026093` (`n=12`). The working-model intervals were
`[0.014236, 0.014372]` and `[0.014321, 0.014804]`. These descriptive intervals
assume independent, approximately normal block log contrasts; sequential work
on one shared host does not establish those assumptions.

LZ4 stored 102,127 independent-unit bytes and 5,284 batch bytes. For the
deterministic pseudorandom control, both codecs selected raw payloads for every
unit: 275,456 stored bytes in independent form and 262,157 in batch form after
the common 13-byte-per-unit header.

The separate no-work process control averaged 1.639 ms in 12 processes before
the timed schedule and 1.721 ms in 12 after it. Startup was not subtracted from
the in-process codec intervals.

## Interpretation limit

This is a hot, single-thread, pinned-processor, in-memory microbenchmark on this
host. It excludes input/output (I/O), cold allocation, dictionaries, contention, tail
latency, energy, and hostile-input exhaustion. The unit-shape treatment changes
call count, framing, history reset, and fallback together. It does not identify
one of those mechanisms in isolation or establish an x86-64 or Intel-family
result.
