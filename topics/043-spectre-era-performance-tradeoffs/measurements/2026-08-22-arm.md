## Result

Each mode ran in 24 fresh processes with 20,000,000 timed lookups per process.
Setup and a 200,000-lookup warmup were excluded. All six execution orders
appeared four times. The A/A control ratio was `0.96797`, with a 95% confidence
interval of `[0.91696, 1.02183]`; it passed the fixed screen.

The descriptive columns report nanoseconds (ns) per lookup. The geometric mean
averages process timings on a multiplicative scale; process log standard
deviation (SD) is the sample SD of their natural logarithms. The ratio intervals

## Observed code and interpretation

The plain path used `cmp`, conditional `b.cs`, then `ldr`. The masked path used
`cmp`, `ngc` (the assembler alias of the `sbc` mask operation), `csdb`, `and`,
and `ldr`; Rust also retained a post-mask bounds branch. The barrier path placed
`dsb nsh` and `isb` after the bounds branch and before `ldr`.

The fixed stream derives about half of its indices outside the array:
9,999,927 of 20,000,000, or 49.999635%. That count is derived from the committed
generator and seed, not a hardware counter. The mask result is therefore a
branch-behavior result for this input, not a claim that mitigation has negative
cost. No branch-miss counter or transient-disclosure test was run.

The kernel reported Spectre Variant 1 user-pointer sanitization, Spectre
Variant 2 Cache Speculation Variant 2 (CSV2) and Branch History Buffer
mitigation, and task-selectable Speculative Store Bypass disablement. Those
strings describe kernel policy; they do not certify this user-space lookup.
